Food safety guidance

  • Donate only food you would happily eat yourself.
  • Cooked food kept at room temperature must be collected within 4 hours of preparation. The form blocks longer windows.
  • Keep hot food above 60°C and chilled food below 5°C until pickup.
  • Use clean, covered containers and mention common allergens (nuts, dairy, gluten, egg).
  • Never list expired, mouldy, leaking or partly eaten food. Expired items are rejected automatically.

This is general guidance, not legal advice. Follow FSSAI and local food-safety rules.

How It Works

Three easy steps from surplus to a shared meal.

  1. 1

    List surplus food

    Donors add what is left, how many servings, when it was made, its use-by time and where to collect it.

  2. 2

    Request food

    NGOs and volunteers search the available listings, check the donor badge and request what they can collect.

  3. 3

    Collect & distribute

    Once collected, the listing is marked as picked up and counted in the impact dashboard.

The problem

Edible food is often thrown away because donors do not know who can collect it in time, while NGOs and volunteers struggle to find out where surplus food is.

Our solution

One simple board. Donors post food with safe-handling details and a pickup deadline. Collectors browse, request and confirm pickup.

Verify Your Account

Choose any method. Each one is saved as Pending Review in this demo.

FSSAI licence or registration

We only check the number's format (14 digits). We cannot confirm the licence with FSSAI in this demo, so it will stay Pending Review.

Identity verification

Upload a government photo ID. We do not ask for the ID number here. A real service (for example an authorised eKYC or DigiLocker provider) would be needed to check it. If you use Aadhaar, upload a masked copy.

NGO registration (for NGOs)

NGOs can submit their registration number and supporting documents. A reviewer would check these against the registrar's records.

Phone / email (OTP)

OTP codes cannot be sent in this demo because no SMS or email service is connected, so no code is checked and the contact stays Pending Review.

Address verification

Submit your address and a proof of address. Only the PIN code and file name are kept in this demo, not the address text.
